# Real-phone test checklist

A short list to try the game on a real **iPhone (Safari)** and a real **Android phone (Chrome)**. Our automatic checks run in a pretend phone on a computer. They can't hear sound, feel a thumb or press a real silent switch, so these need a person and a real phone.

**First, the game has to be on a website.** Phones can't open the game by double-clicking a file. That needs your go-ahead. Suggested: **itch.io** (free; the page can be kept private behind a password while testing) or **GitHub Pages** (free, but the game and its files are public, including unreleased art). Once it's up, you'll get a link to open on each phone.

Turn the phone's volume up first. Tick each line, or write what went wrong in the notes at the bottom.

## 1. Opening it
- [ ] Open the link. Holding the phone **upright** (phones only; a tablet plays upright with no message), a "TURN YOUR PHONE SIDEWAYS" message appears and the game waits.
- [ ] Turn the phone **sideways**. The message goes away. The picture is big and sharp (no blurry pixels), with buttons beside it.
- [ ] (Optional) Turn back upright and tap **PLAY UPRIGHT ANYWAY**. The game shows above the buttons. Turn sideways again to carry on.

## 2. Sound
- [ ] Tap **JUMP** (or tap the picture) to start. **Sound plays from that first tap**: the start sound, then the music.
- [ ] **iPhone silent switch** (the small switch on the side): try it both ways.
  - No switch? On newer iPhones (iPhone 15 Pro and later), open Control Center (swipe down from the top-right corner) and tap the bell to turn Silent mode on and off. Silent mode on shows a bell with a line through it.
  - Today the sound effects are computer-made beeps. So is the music on the website copy: the owner's theme song plays only in the owner's own copy until its license is confirmed (owner question 21). With the silent switch **on** (orange showing, or the crossed-out bell), an iPhone may play **no sound at all**. That's how iPhones treat this kind of sound, not a game fault. Turn Silent mode off to hear it.
  - Real sound files (such as the theme song, once it's in the website copy) should play even with the silent switch on. Note what you hear.
- [ ] Sound effects play when you jump and whip, and they don't cut out after a while.

## 3. Playing
- [ ] Move left and right with the arrow pad. Slide your thumb across the middle to turn round without lifting it.
- [ ] Tap JUMP for a small hop. Hold JUMP for a higher jump.
- [ ] WHIP does the tail-whip. POSSUM plays dead.
- [ ] Move and jump at the same time with two thumbs.
- [ ] Buttons feel big enough, and no button covers Sammy or a zombie walking on the grass.

## 4. Pause, restart, mute, full screen
- [ ] The pause button pauses. RESTART and MUTE show while paused.
- [ ] MUTE silences everything. Tap it again (it says UNMUTE) and sound comes back.
- [ ] RESTART starts the level again.
- [ ] **Android:** the full-screen button (four corners, next to pause) hides the browser's bars. Tap it again to leave full screen. (iPhone has no full-screen button; use Add to Home Screen below instead.)
- [ ] **iPhone:** in Safari tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Open "Sammy" from the home screen. It should fill the whole screen with no browser bars. Check sound starts on the first tap there too.

## 5. Interruptions
- [ ] **Lock the phone** mid-level, then unlock it. The game is **paused** and stays paused. Tap play: the game carries on and **the sound comes back**.
- [ ] **Switch to another app** (or get a friend to call you) mid-level, then come back. Same as above: paused, and sound returns after you unpause.
- [ ] If sound doesn't come back after unpausing, tap the screen once more and note whether that fixes it.

## 6. The screen
- [ ] On a phone with a **notch** or camera hole: turn the phone both ways sideways. Nothing important (hearts, timer, buttons) is hidden by the notch, rounded corners or the home bar.
- [ ] **No accidental zoom:** double-tap quickly on the picture and on the buttons, and try pinching. The page should not zoom in or move.
- [ ] Long-pressing a button doesn't bring up a "copy" or "save image" menu.

## 7. The whole level
- [ ] Play through to the **gauntlet** (the fenced fight at the end) and finish the level.
- [ ] Note anything that felt too hard, too easy, slow or jerky.
